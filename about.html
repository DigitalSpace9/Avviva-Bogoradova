About Me



Within each canvas, I search for myself, striving to unravel the enigma of my own existence. I seek to merge with the boundless void, ultimately dissolving into it like a singular atom. Yet, even in this emptiness, in this absolute unity, I remain myself.

That is why, when you look at my paintings, you see more than just paint on canvas. You see a reflection of your own soul, your own universe, hidden beyond the visible.

I believe that each of us is a universe compressed into a micron, a microcosm teeming with cosmic energy resonating in harmony with the vast unknown. My paintings are a reflection of this inner universe, an attempt to illuminate its hidden secrets.
